# Applied AI

An AI engineering company in Przemyśl, Poland. We build the full stack, from the cluster and the services to the models, so AI survives contact with production. Two engineers: Paweł Rojek (CEO) and Piotr Rojek (CTO).

## Products

- [CityChat](https://citychat.pl): a city guide you talk to, live on iOS and Android in 16 cities in Poland and Spain.
- [SanAlert](https://sanalert.pl): unofficial early warning about air threats over Poland, with the official RCB and RSO messages in their original wording.
- [FormFlow](https://formflow.solutions): contact forms for consumer brands, with every submitted photo checked for authenticity.
- [JPK Doctor](https://jpk-doctor.pl): tells an accountant whether the tax authority's gateway will accept a SAF-T file. Runs offline.

## Open source

- [polish-law-checker](https://github.com/appliedai-pl/polish-law-checker): checks the Polish-law citations in a document against the official text from the Sejm's API. A Claude Code skill.
- [sanalert-mcp](https://github.com/plrojek/sanalert-mcp): SanAlert's level for a place, for AI assistants, worked out on the user's own computer.
- [sanalert-home-assistant](https://github.com/plrojek/sanalert-home-assistant): SanAlert in Home Assistant.

From the team: [agent-skills](https://github.com/plrojek/agent-skills), Paweł's Claude Code plugins, and [garden-planner](https://github.com/plrojek/garden-planner).
